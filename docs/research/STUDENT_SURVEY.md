# Survey: How students learn using YouTube

Created: 2026-10-11. Draft for SurveySwap or another form builder; not published.

## Description

We’re exploring how college students in India use YouTube to understand topics, find related explanations, and resolve questions. This survey is for people aged 18 or older who currently study at a college or university in India and have used YouTube to learn something in the past 30 days. There are no right or wrong answers. Please answer based on your actual experiences.

Participation is voluntary. Please do not include names or other identifying information in your written answers. Any contact information for follow-up is optional.

Add an estimated completion time after testing the finished form. Do not promise anonymity without checking the form platform's data collection settings.

## Analysis notes

- Test the form with 2–3 people for clarity, branching, and completion time before publishing.
- Report recruitment source, eligibility exclusions, and denominators. SurveySwap respondents are a self-selected sample, not representative of all Indian students.
- Analyze curiosity-driven learners separately from exam, work, and other goals.
- Tool switching alone is not evidence of a problem. Examine reasons for switching, difficulty, unmet needs, and incomplete goals together.
- Examine unrelated viewing alongside whether participants returned to learning; distinguish intentional stopping from unwanted derailment.
- Concept usefulness and feature choices are stated preferences. Validate them through actual prototype use and voluntary return.
- The survey does not measure willingness to pay or prove learning improvement. The initial product is intended to be free.
