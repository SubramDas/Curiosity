About this demo

Curiosity is an experimental learning app submitted to AI Builder Cup 2026. It is intended for adult learners aged 18 or older. Search results, entertaining-break candidates and AI explanations are not independently reviewed for accuracy or suitability. Watching a video does not establish mastery.

Your information

  • Guest topics and playback positions are saved in your browser. Clear the site's browser data to remove them from that device. Signing out returns to separate guest data; it does not delete account records.
  • If you create an account, Supabase processes your email, password and sign-in session. Saved account topics and playback positions are stored in Supabase under user access rules.
  • Questions, recent conversation context and the selected video's public URL/title may be sent to Google's Gemini service for explanations and video analysis. Avoid entering sensitive personal or confidential information. This app keeps the conversation in memory and clears it on refresh, a new conversation or an account change.
  • Search text and optional learning goals may be sent to Gemini to check learning intent. Public video titles, descriptions and tags may also be checked to filter educational results and related breaks. Avoid sensitive or confidential information in searches. Decisions and generated search alternatives are cached for up to six hours; public-video purpose checks are cached for up to 24 hours. Alternatives may reflect submitted wording.
  • Accepted searches request results from YouTube. Videos play through the official YouTube player. Public video metadata is temporarily cached in Firestore; cache lookup keys are hashed. Daily service-use counters are retained to enforce budgets.
  • Google Cloud serves this website. Its infrastructure and the external services may maintain their own operational records under their policies. The app does not deliberately log question bodies, email addresses or passwords.

External services and limitations

YouTube videos belong to their creators. Google, YouTube and Supabase apply their own service terms and data policies. AI can make mistakes, and suggested video timestamps can be approximate. Daily limits and temporary outages may make new searches or answers unavailable.

This is a demonstration, not a validated assessment system. For account-data or support requests, contact the project team through the hackathon submission contact details. Account deletion is not yet available as an in-app button.
